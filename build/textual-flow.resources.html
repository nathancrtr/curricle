Suggested reading order

The curriculum formalizes this; here it is at a glance.

  1. Wasserman & Gurry (fast skim, then chapter-by-chapter through Phase 1)
  2. Parker, selectively (alongside Unit 1)
  3. Mink, pass 1 (Unit 3) — with the Genealogical Queries guide + live tool
  4. Gurry's Critical Examination + Mink pass 2 + the Carlson/Mink exchange (Phase 2)
  5. Handbook of Stemmatology ch. 5 + the Camps papers (Phases 2–3)
  6. Taming the BEAST + McCollum & Turnbull (+ McElreath as needed) (Phase 4)
  7. Royse + Metzger's Commentary + Willker (Phase 6)
  8. Greek: Decker continuously from week 0; STEP Bible from Phase 3; Wallace from Phase 5
